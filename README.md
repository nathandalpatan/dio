# DIO

**Words, understood.**

DIO is a lightweight, system-wide dictionary for your desktop. Highlight a word in any app, press a shortcut, and a small pop-up shows its definition right next to it. No browser, no switching windows.

## How it works

1. Highlight a word anywhere on your computer.
2. Trigger DIO with a keyboard shortcut or the right-click menu.
3. A compact pop-up appears beside the word with:
   - the word and its pronunciation (with audio)
   - part of speech
   - a short definition
   - optional examples and synonyms
4. Click **More** for details, or dismiss it and keep working.

## Status

Early concept. The first version targets macOS, using the Accessibility APIs to read selected text and a global keyboard shortcut to trigger lookups.
